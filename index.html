About Me My Work

Inspired by Human Centric Design

I'm a full-stack software engineer with a core belief that good technology should be intuitive to use, thoughtful by design, and of high quality. With a background in Computer Science and Disability Studies, I strive to continue learning, while empowering others.

Currently, I'm a Software Engineer at Cephable. We're building a private, On-device desktop assistant that helps people work across their apps - keeping data private and costs low. When I am not coding, I'm on adventourous hikes, playing ice hockey, trying new recipes or reading a book.
