My story

The person behind the code

I'm Shristi — a full-stack software engineer based in Cambridge, MA, currently a Software Engineer II at Cephable, where we build technology that helps people interact with their devices on their own terms through alternative input methods.

My path into tech was shaped by an unexpected combination: a Computer Science degree paired with a minor in Critical Disability Studies from UT Austin. That pairing wasn't an accident — it gave me a framework for thinking about technology that goes beyond what it does to who it serves. Accessibility has been at the center of my work ever since.

Before Cephable, I was a full-stack engineer and Platform Accessibility Representative at Epic Systems, building the kind of UI infrastructure that healthcare workers use every single day. Before that, a handful of internships at Truework, Coursera, and Relativity — each of which taught me something different about how software gets built (and sometimes broken).

Outside of work, I try to stay curious and stay moving. I'm the person who will spend a whole weekend on a trail, sign up for an adult beginner hockey league and never look back, recreate a restaurant dish from memory on a Tuesday, and start a new book before finishing the last one.
