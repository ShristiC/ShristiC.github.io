My Story

I'm Shristi — a full-stack software engineer based in Cambridge, MA, currently working at Cephable. We're building a private, on-device desktop assistant that helps people work across their apps — keeping data private and costs low.

I have a background in Computer Science and Disability Studies from UT Austin. That pairing led me to prioritize the end-user experience, thoughtful scale, and high-quality code. Focusing on the who and the why has given me opportunities to make large-scale impact while keeping pace with a fast-changing industry.

A few things I've worked on at Cephable:

  • Internationalization — 5 languages, expanded our user base into Canada, LATAM, Europe, and Japan.
  • Cross-app detection — 200+ integrations across Google, Microsoft, Adobe, and more, reducing manual setup and speeding up onboarding.
  • License management — trial, upgrade, and downgrade flows for resellers, organizations, and individual users, streamlining admin and boosting paid conversions.
  • Multi-device connections — Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 pilot integration, which drove 20K+ users in 3 months (a 10x increase).
  • Screen context awareness — native Mac and Windows implementations with no screen recording, giving performant context to AI workflows and voice-mouse accessibility (Dragon compete)
  • Onboarding tutorials — 30+ step-by-step guides, Scribes, and use-case videos, plus in-app contextual tutorials, for faster onboarding and less friction.
  • Automated testing infrastructure — higher app quality and accountability which increased credibility with B2B partners and end users.

For a deeper dive, check out my Tech page.

Before Cephable, I was a full-stack engineer and Platform Accessibility Representative at Epic Systems (EHR). I worked on Hyperspace, the core infrastructure team serving multiple departments and thousands of healthcare systems. During college, I had a handful of internships at Truework, Coursera, and Relativity.

Outside of work, I try to stay curious and stay moving. My evenings are spent either cooking or playing ice hockey. And my weekends, either socializing, reading, or hiking / camping / motorcycling outdoors. I love to travel and am a BIG foodie.
